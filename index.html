Where we're staying

Villa Ketty

Perched above Vico Equense on the Sorrento Peninsula, Villa Ketty is a family-run resort with panoramic views of the Gulf of Naples, Castello Giusso, and Mount Vesuvius. Our home for four nights — and the place Antonio and Denise make feel like family from the first espresso.

  • 4 nights · Check-in Tue Aug 11 · Check-out Fri Aug 15
  • Address · Via Comunale Scraio 10, Vico Equense
  • Breakfast · Fresh Km0 buffet daily, 8:00–10:30 AM
  • Dinner · Most evenings at the hotel — no fixed menu, always seasonal
  • Amenities · Pool, terraces, shuttle to town, free parking

Planned day trips

Matera

UNESCO World Heritage site — the ancient Sassi cave dwellings carved into the hillside. One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world.

Ostuni

The "White City" perched on a hilltop, with sweeping views over olive groves to the Adriatic Sea. Wandering its maze of alleys is half the fun.

Alberobello

Iconic conical trulli roofs as far as the eye can see. A UNESCO site and the postcard image of Puglia.

Flexible days

When we're not on a day trip

The remaining days in Puglia are ours to shape. Pick what sounds best — or do nothing at all.

Beach days

Torre Canne, Rosa Marina, or the Adriatic coast — crystal water and fresh seafood.

Nearby towns

Locorotondo, Martina Franca, Polignano a Mare, Monopoli — each worth an afternoon.

Olive bike tour

Cycle through the Valle d'Itria olive groves — Puglia produces more olive oil than anywhere in Italy.

Slow days at home

Market runs in Cisternino, cooking together, aperitivo on the terrace. No agenda required.
